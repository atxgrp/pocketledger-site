Sources and credits

Data sources

  • Exchange rates: reference rates of the European Central Bank, published at https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/eurofxref/ (eurofxref-daily.xml). Source: ECB.
  • Cryptocurrency prices: data provided by CoinGecko (https://www.coingecko.com), through its public API.
  • Bank connections (optional): GoCardless Bank Account Data (https://bankaccountdata.gocardless.com) and Enable Banking (https://enablebanking.com), with your own access keys. Their terms apply.
  • Carbon footprint: per-category factors are orders of magnitude set by the publisher, editable by you; they do not come from an official database and do not replace a carbon audit.
  • Default categories, report templates and texts: works by ATX Grp.

Supported formats and standards

CSV, OFX/QFX, QIF, MT940, CAMT.053 (ISO 20022), HomeBank export files (.xhb, .hbk) and Actual Budget databases, read-only, following their public specifications. No data or code from those projects is included.

Cryptography

SHA-256, PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (RFC 8018), AES-256-GCM and ECDSA P-256 signatures (FIPS 186-4), provided by Apple's CryptoKit and CommonCrypto; update signatures (EdDSA) are checked by Sparkle.

Components

The distributed version embeds a single third-party library: Sparkle (https://sparkle-project.org), which checks and installs updates, under the MIT licence with the licences of its components (full text: https://github.com/sparkle-project/Sparkle/blob/2.x/LICENSE). No other open-source code is included. The app uses Apple frameworks shipped with macOS: SwiftUI, Swift Charts, CryptoKit, CloudKit, WidgetKit, App Intents, Vision, PDFKit, EventKit, LocalAuthentication, Security, Foundation and SQLite (reading Actual Budget files). Pictograms are Apple SF Symbols, used under their licence. The icon and visual identity are creations of ATX Grp.

Frequently asked questions

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. It stays on your device. The app only contacts the services you turn on, such as iCloud for syncing.

Is Pocket Ledger really free?

Yes: no paid tier, no ads, no data sold.

When is it out?

The apps are being tested before their release. Write to us to be told when it is out.

How do I import my bank statements?

An assistant reads your file (CSV, OFX, QIF, MT940, CAMT.053), lets you fix each row and flags duplicates. PayPal and Trade Republic have their own import.

Can I use it on several devices?

Yes. iCloud sync links Mac, iPhone and iPad, attachments included; an iCloud Drive, Dropbox or Syncthing folder works too.

Does Pocket Ledger connect to my bank?

Not directly: you import your statements. An optional connection exists for people who want to set it up themselves.

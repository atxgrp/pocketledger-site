Frequently asked questions

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. It stays on your Mac. The app only contacts the services you turn on.

Is Pocket Ledger really free?

Yes: no paid tier, no ads, no data sold.

How do I import my bank statements?

An assistant reads your CSV, lets you fix each row and flags duplicates. OFX, QIF, MT940 and CAMT.053 are read too.

Can I use it on several Macs?

Yes: pick an iCloud Drive, Dropbox or Syncthing folder, the app reloads the file when it changes.

And on iPhone?

Under construction: the iPhone and iPad app is written and still needs trying on real devices before a beta. See the roadmap.

What’s new

The release log.

1.2.0

New

  • CSV import assistant: columns to check, preview with totals, remembered bank profiles.
  • Subscriptions detected in your history, with monthly and yearly cost.
  • Attachments gathered in one screen, with search, sorting and quick preview.
  • Shared accounts with per-person rights, protected by signatures.
  • Go to… (⌘K), back and forward between screens, quick entry from the menu bar.
  • Daily automatic backups, due-date reminders, optional Spotlight.
  • Hide amounts everywhere (⇧⌘H) and "Erase all my data".
  • Automatic updates (off until you turn them on), About, full help.
  • Integrity check with repairs, and a change journal (undo one specific change).
  • Fingerprinted backups: preview before restoring, verified automatic copies.
  • Import you can fix row by row, duplicates flagged, a tag to find a batch again.
  • Discovery mode with removable samples, a "What's new" screen after an update.
  • Privacy: amounts hidden at launch or in the background, windows hidden from captures.
  • Adjustable text size, stronger contrast, notifications with buttons (confirm, skip).
  • Import: bank labels are cleaned up ("CARTE CARREFOUR MARKET" becomes "Carrefour Market", the original label is kept in the memo) and a category is suggested from your rules, your history, then a few well-known names.
  • "To review" screen after an import, categories that follow your corrections, merging of names from the same brand.
  • Share an account through iCloud: one profile per person (name, e-mail, rights) and a ready-to-send e-mail invitation.
  • Loans: compare "shorten the term" with "lower the payment". "Where the money goes" widget with a + button.
  • Forecast: your balance month by month from what is planned, what is regular, your loans and an estimate of everyday spending.
  • Analysing your data: transfers to link, categories to merge, subscriptions, loans, balance and suggested budgets.
  • Recurring items, Subscriptions and Loans suggest what is already in your accounts, and can start from a recorded payment.
  • PayPal import: one payment per purchase with its shop, linked to your bank's PayPal lines.
  • Current balance at import, statement order kept within a day, sortable columns.
  • Import: row-by-row preview of the category and name your rules will apply.
  • Transaction status chosen with a click, tag added to a selection, new tag applied to the transactions you pick.
  • Savings and investment accounts: Livret A, LDD, LEP, PEL, PEA, life insurance, Trade Republic… with ceiling, rate, interest and value updates.
  • Bank on each account (list of banks with a badge), and asked at import: CSV layout found per bank.
  • Trade Republic import: export recognised, deposits, fees, dividends and interest net of tax, account created, portfolio value or cash as you choose.
  • Loans: "constant total instalment" like bank amortisation tables (insurance as a % of remaining capital, fixed debit).
  • Loans: original loan (start, capital, term), instalments paid and status of each instalment; term typed as a number of months.
  • Analysis: tracked loans leave subscriptions and are listed in the analysis, confirmation before bulk clearing, readable months, link to the forecast.
  • Fixes: the total of "All transactions" ignores closed accounts; transfers to PayPal no longer look like a duplicate of the purchase ("Clarify" button in the analysis).
  • Payees: alphabetical sort (or by number of transactions) and search; a "Decline" button to keep some names from being merged.
  • Import: identical lines within one file and internal transfers with no other leg are imported with a flag; large OFX files are read about six times faster; widget captions have more contrast.

Compatibility

  • macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later; Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.

Stability

  • Charts cope with unusual data (refunds, zero or huge amounts).
  • Every backup is read back after writing and refused if it is not right.
  • A data file newer than the app is never overwritten.

Fixed

  • "12 50" is refused instead of being read as 1250.
  • CSV import opens the next screen after the assistant.
  • Siri and Shortcuts can no longer replace an unreadable file with an empty ledger.

To test

  • Import a CSV statement from your bank and save the profile.
  • Type "12 50" in an amount and check it is refused.
  • Hide amounts (⇧⌘H) and browse every screen.
  • Make a backup and restore it from the File menu.
  • Manage › Ledger hygiene: each "View" opens the exact lines concerned, with the "Show all" banner.
  • Categories: icons, Pets category, click a category to see its transactions.
  • Editing one leg of a linked transfer (date, amount, note) updates the other; the merchant name is kept.
  • Category icons in lists, budgets and menus.
  • Register: select two lines from different accounts → "Link these two lines".
  • Example prompts in the transaction fields; click a recent transaction on the dashboard.
  • Simpler backups: automatic copies on this Mac and the iCloud copy only (File → Backups…). Settings button removed from the top bar.
  • Link two lines: the purchase and the debit share name, category, note and tags.
  • New: Housing screen (Manage › Housing): property type, situation (rental, purchase with a loan, owned), costs by item, linked loan, statistics and tips.
  • Graphite dark theme (no more pure black), calmer interface: lighter figures, neutral icons, Net worth card without gradient.
  • iPhone: automatic copies on the device and a Backups screen; link a line from its details; graphite background on every list.
  • Debits linked to a PayPal purchase open in the full editor (name, category, status…).
  • "To review" only counts transactions that still exist.

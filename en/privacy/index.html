Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026. Publisher: ATX Grp — atxgrp@gmail.com

In short

Pocket Ledger runs on your Mac. Your accounts, transactions, budgets and attachments stay with you. ATX Grp receives none of your data: no account to create, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party analytics service.

Where your data lives

  • A readable file (ledger.json) and an attachments folder, in ~/Library/Application Support/PocketLedger or in the folder you choose in Settings → Data.
  • The file can only be read by your macOS user (permissions 600).
  • Bank access keys and your shared-account signing keys are kept in your Mac's Keychain, never in the data file.
  • Automatic copies of the file (one per day, 14 days and one per month) are kept in ~/Library/Application Support/PocketLedger/Backups, with the same protections. You can turn them off in Settings → Data.
  • A change journal (what changed and when) is kept on this Mac only, in ~/Library/Application Support/PocketLedger/Journal; "Erase all my data" deletes it too.
  • Backups can be password-encrypted (AES-256-GCM). Without the password nobody, ATX Grp included, can read or recover them.

Network connections

The app only goes online in these cases:

  • Exchange rates: only if one of your accounts is in a foreign currency (or when you click "Update"), and at most every 12 hours, the app downloads the reference rates published by the European Central Bank. No personal data is sent; your IP address is visible to the ECB as with any web page.
  • Cryptocurrency prices: only if you enter cryptocurrencies. The app asks CoinGecko for prices; only the symbols of tracked assets are sent, along with your IP address.
  • Updates (optional, off by default): if you turn on automatic checking or click "Check now", the app contacts the publisher's server (its address is shown in Settings → General). Only the app version and the update library's version are sent, along with your IP address; no profile of your Mac is sent. The feed is hosted on GitHub Pages: GitHub then receives your IP address, under its own privacy policy.
  • Bank connection: only if you set it up. Your transactions travel between your bank, the provider you chose (GoCardless or Enable Banking) and your Mac, using keys you created yourself with that provider. ATX Grp is not in the loop.
  • iCloud: only if you turn synchronisation on. Your data is then stored in your private iCloud space (CloudKit), run by Apple under its own policy. ATX Grp has no access.
  • Shared folder or shared account: if you put data in a synced folder (iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Syncthing…) or share an account, it is visible to the people and the service that manage that folder.

System permissions

  • Calendar: only if you turn on showing your scheduled items in the Calendar app. The app writes to a "Pocket Ledger" calendar and does not read your other events.
  • Touch ID / password: locking uses the system; the app never receives your fingerprint or password.
  • Receipt scanning: text recognition runs on your Mac (Vision); the image never leaves the device.
  • Siri, Shortcuts, widgets, menu-bar quick entry: everything stays local on your Mac.
  • Notifications: reminders (due items, envelopes, balances) are local notifications; nothing is sent to a server.
  • Spotlight (optional, off by default): if you turn it on, the payees, amounts and categories of your transactions are added to your Mac's search index, visible even while the app is locked. Turning the option off erases that index.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays as long as you keep it. To erase everything in one go: Settings → Data → Erase all my data… (after confirmation, the app deletes your data, attachments, copies, Keychain keys, Spotlight index, reminders and settings on this Mac, as well as the iCloud copy if sync was on). You can also quit the app and delete its data folder by hand.

Your rights

ATX Grp holds no data from your financial ledger. You stay in control: you can export (CSV, QIF, backup) and delete your data at any time. If you write to us, we use your message and e-mail address only to reply. To exercise your rights (access, rectification, erasure, objection) or ask a question: atxgrp@gmail.com. You may also contact your country's data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Changes

This policy may change with the app; the update date is shown at the top.

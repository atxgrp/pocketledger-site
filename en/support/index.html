Frequently asked questions

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. It stays on your device. The app only contacts the services you turn on (exchange rates, crypto prices, bank connection, updates).

Is Pocket Ledger really free?

Yes: no paid tier, no ads.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, on Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.

Which iPhones and iPads does it run on?

iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. The app is in TestFlight testing before its release.

How do I import my bank statements?

Accounts → Import: an assistant reads a CSV's columns and lets you fix them row by row before importing. OFX, QIF, MT940 and CAMT.053 are read too. Duplicates are flagged and an imported batch can be undone entirely.

How do I sync several devices?

iCloud sync links Mac, iPhone and iPad. On the Mac, Settings → Data also lets you pick an iCloud Drive, Dropbox or Syncthing folder: the app reloads the file when it changes.

How do I share an account with someone?

Right-click the account → Share… You set each person's rights: read, enter or change everything.

How do I back up and restore?

Two backups only: automatic copies on your Mac (File → Backups…) and the iCloud copy (Settings → Data).

I made a mistake: how do I go back?

Edit → Undo (⌥⌘Z) reverses the last change. Manage → Change journal can undo one specific change.

What if the app reports a damaged file?

Manage → Integrity check repairs it without losing anything; otherwise restore a backup.

How do I erase all my data?

Settings → Your data → Erase all my data.

I cannot find an update.

Until a version is published, the app says there is none. Updates are off until you turn them on.

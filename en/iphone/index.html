What the iPhone adds

  • Quick entry: amount, payee, category, done.
  • Face ID to lock the app and hide amounts.
  • Widgets and Siri: net worth on the Home Screen, an expense by voice.
  • Budgets, forecast and loans as on the Mac.
  • iCloud sync with your Mac.

Also on iPad

The same app runs on iPad, with more room for your lists.

iPhone Duo

Two displays, one app.

Pocket Ledger follows the size of the screen, not the model of the device: on the outer display and on the inner one, the layout adapts, in portrait and in landscape.

  • No fixed width: the layout relies on the room available and on safe areas, so nothing slips under the hinge or a cut-out.
  • Same features on both displays: accounts, budgets, forecast, loans and quick entry.
  • System bars: the standard tab bar and toolbars, which keep their usual placement.
  • Tested in Apple's simulator on both displays, in portrait and in landscape.

Requirements

iOS 17 or later. The apps are in TestFlight testing before their release.

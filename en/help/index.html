Getting started

Pocket Ledger requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, on Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.

1. Create an account (Accounts → New account): name, currency, starting balance. One account per bank, card, savings book or cash box.

2. Add transactions with "New transaction" (⌘N), or import your bank statement (Accounts → Import).

3. Let Pocket Ledger suggest categories: it learns from your rules and history.

4. Set envelopes in Budget, then follow the dashboard.

Tip: ⌘K opens "Go to…" to reach any screen or account by typing a few letters; ⌘[ and ⌘] go back and forward like a browser.

Accounts

Types: bank account, card, cash, savings, asset, liability. You can group accounts ("Family", "Savings"…), set a minimum balance (an alert shows on the dashboard), close an account without deleting it and give each account its own currency. Right-click an account: Edit, Share.

Transactions

  • Quick entry: amount, date, payee, category. Expense or income in one click.
  • "Advanced": tags, split across several categories, attachments and receipt scanning.
  • Statuses: pending, cleared (seen on the statement), reconciled (checked). The flag is a reminder.
  • Transfers between accounts, including different currencies.
  • Multi-selection: categorise, clear, flag or delete at once (a confirmation is asked before deleting). Undo: ⌥⌘Z.
  • Quick filters above the list, free search and detailed filter (period, amount, category, tag…).

Categories, tags, payees

Hierarchical categories (income or expense, "need" or "want" for the 50/30/20 view). Cross-cutting tags group by theme (#holiday). Manage → Payees: double-click a name to rename it (all its transactions and future imports follow); it also merges names that mean the same merchant (CARD 12/03 CARREFOUR → Carrefour) and suggests the obvious groupings, including names that share a brand ("Carrefour Market" and "Carrefour City").

Automatic rules

Manage → Rules: "if the payee or memo contains… then category…". Rules apply to entries and imports; you can limit a rule to expenses, income or one account, and apply it to history.

Budget

One envelope per category and month; the mark on the bar shows where the month stands. Leftovers can roll over. The assistant suggests amounts from your last 3 months, and you can move money between envelopes. A message warns you when the current pace will exceed an envelope.

Recurring items

Rent, subscriptions, salaries: due date, frequency, amount. Confirm with one click or turn on automatic posting. Upcoming items show on the dashboard and, if you wish, in the Calendar app (Settings → General).

Loans

Manage → Loans: principal, annual rate, term, insurance. The app computes the schedule, interest, remaining principal, simulates an early repayment and can create the recurring payment.

Subscriptions

Manage → Subscriptions: payments that come back at the same rhythm with a steady amount (three times, or twice for a yearly payment), their cost per month and per year, and the ones that seem cancelled. "Plan" creates the recurring item.

Savings goals

A target amount and a date: the app tells you how much to set aside each month and your progress.

Currencies

Foreign-currency accounts are converted with the ECB reference rates (Manage → Currencies → Update). Without a rate for a currency, its accounts are left out of totals and a warning appears.

Cryptocurrencies

Manage → Crypto: buys, sells, fees, quantity; valuation with CoinGecko prices, unrealised gain, threshold alerts. Prices can also be entered by hand.

Reports

Monthly, breakdown, 50/30/20 structure, trend, yearly, cash-flow projection, financial health and carbon footprint. Choose the period (12 months, 24 months or custom), filter by category, print or export to PDF. The "Create" menu opens the Builder (your own report: grouping, measure, filters, saving) and Simulations ("what if…?", savings, loan, financial runway).

Attachments

In a transaction → Advanced: attach a file or scan a receipt (the text is read on your Mac). Manage → Attachments lists every file: search, column sorting, filter by type or account, open, show in Finder, go to the transaction, detach.

Sharing an account

Right-click an account (Mac) or long-press it (iPhone) → Share… Two methods. Through iCloud (recommended): create one profile per person (first name, last name, the e-mail address of their Apple ID, rights), tap "Create the share", then "Invite by e-mail": your mail app opens with the message and the link ready. The person opens the link on a device where Pocket Ledger is installed, and the account appears there with no further question. Through a folder the other person can open (shared iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Syncthing). In both cases, add them and set their rights: read only, entry (they edit their own lines) or edit everything. With a folder, the guest chooses "Join a shared account…". Every change is signed: nobody can give themselves more rights or forge the file. Compare the key shown on each side by phone. Only this account is shared.

Import and export

On import, names the bank wrote in capitals are cleaned up (a switch keeps the original names) and a category is suggested: your rules first, then your history, then a few well-known names (supermarkets, fuel, subscriptions…). The original label stays in the memo. Once the import is done, the "To review" screen (Manage → Data quality) lists the added transactions with the suggested category: fix it if needed, then confirm. For a given name, the category you chose last is the one used next.

CSV import: an assistant shows the recognised columns (date, amount or debit/credit, description…), lets you fix them, flip the sign or read dates as month/day, and previews the result with totals before importing. "Save" remembers a bank's layout: it then applies by itself. Other formats: OFX/QFX, QIF, MT940, CAMT.053, HomeBank (.xhb, .hbk), Actual Budget. Duplicates are detected: re-importing creates none. Export: CSV (all transactions or one account), QIF. A watched folder (Settings → Data) automatically imports statements dropped in it.

Automatic backups

Settings → Data: one copy of the file per day (refreshed hourly), the last 14 days and one copy per month, on this Mac only. "View and restore…" brings a copy back; your current data is kept aside.

Hide amounts

The eye button in the top bar (⇧⌘H) replaces every amount with dots: screens, tables, reminders, widgets and Siri's answers. Charts are hidden (their axes would give the figures away). File exports and PDFs keep the real amounts.

Integrity check and journal

Manage → Data quality. The integrity check looks for damage (duplicates, missing categories, orphan transfers…) and repairs it without losing anything; Edit → Undo reverses a repair. The change journal lists what changed and when, and can undo one specific change as long as its transactions have not been touched since. It stays on this Mac.

Discovery mode

On the welcome screen, "Try with samples" fills the app with fictional data; "Remove samples" takes them out without touching your own data.

Backup and restore

File → Back up data…: full copy, with an optional password (AES-256). Every backup is read back after writing: if it is not right, it is refused and deleted instead of being reported as done. File → Restore a backup… reloads a copy (a file holding transactions without an account is flagged); your current data is kept aside. Settings → Data → Erase all my data… deletes everything, after a double confirmation. A forgotten password cannot be recovered.

Syncing several Macs

Two ways: turn on iCloud (Settings → Data), or put the data folder in a synced folder (iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Syncthing). Simultaneous changes are merged item by item. On a new Mac, "Restore from iCloud" is offered at first launch.

Security

Settings → Security: lock with Touch ID or the Mac password, with a delay. The data file is readable only by your user. Bank keys live in the Keychain. pocketledger://add links always ask for your confirmation.

Updates

Settings → General → Updates: automatic checking stays off until you turn it on; "Check now" and the Pocket Ledger → Check for Updates… menu item check on demand. Tick "Also receive beta versions" to try new things before everyone else. Every update is signed: the app refuses a modified archive. If the section says updates are not enabled, download the new version from the publisher.

Reminders, menu bar and Spotlight

Settings → General: reminders the day before a due item (9 am), when an envelope is overspent or an account falls below its minimum; quick entry from the menu bar; Spotlight search of your transactions (off by default). In Attachments, the space bar opens a quick preview.

Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts

Add the Net worth and Budgets widgets to the Desktop or Notification Centre. Tell Siri "Add an expense in Pocket Ledger", or use the app's Shortcuts actions. The "Where the money goes" widget shows the month's breakdown, with a + button to enter a transaction. pocketledger://open?screen=budget links open a given screen.

Keyboard shortcuts

⌘N new transaction · ⌘K go to… · ⌘1 to ⌘5 tabs · ⌘[ and ⌘] back and forward · ⌥⌘Z undo · ⇧⌥⌘Z redo · ⌘, settings · ⌘? help.

Common problems

  • The app will not open on an older Mac: it needs macOS 14 or later.
  • An amount shows in the wrong currency: check the account's currency (Edit account) and the main currency (Settings → General).
  • Accounts are missing from totals: update the rates (Manage → Currencies).
  • Import creates duplicates: pick the right account before importing; exact duplicates are skipped.
  • iCloud unavailable: it needs the app signed with Xcode and an iCloud session (System Settings).
  • A share file cannot be found: the shared folder has not synced to this Mac yet.
  • Unreadable data at launch: the app sets the file aside (ledger.unreadable-…) without overwriting it; restore a backup.

Glossary

Envelope: amount planned for a category over a month. Rollover: a month's leftover added to the next. Clearing: marking a transaction as seen on the statement. Reconciliation: checking the balance matches the statement. Split: dividing a transaction across several categories. Unrealised gain: profit not yet realised.

Accessibility

Where we stand, honestly.

We want Pocket Ledger to be usable by as many people as possible. This page says honestly where we stand.

What the app offers

Text size

Settings → Display → Text size enlarges key figures and body text. Screens are tested at an enlarged size.

Contrast

Theme colours are adjusted to keep enough contrast in light and dark mode.

Keyboard

Main actions have a shortcut (⌘N, ⌘K "Go to…", ⌘[ and ⌘], ⌥⌘Z) and the app can be driven from the keyboard.

VoiceOver and reduced motion

The app relies on standard macOS components, which support VoiceOver, and on the system's accessibility settings. Icon buttons carry a label.

On-screen privacy

Hiding amounts (⇧⌘H) also helps when using the app in public.

Status

The app's accessibility has not been audited externally: this is a self-assessment. We consider it partial and do not claim conformance with a specific standard.

Known limits

  • Some charts are visual first; their figures are also available as a table or an export.
  • Exported PDFs depend on what you enter.
  • We have not tested every VoiceOver combination on every screen.

Contact us

Having trouble? Write to atxgrp@gmail.com describing what blocks you: we treat it as a priority.

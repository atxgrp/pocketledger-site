Accessibility

Where we stand, honestly.

I want Pocket Ledger to be usable by as many people as possible. This page says honestly where I stand.

What the app offers

Text size

Settings → Display → Text size enlarges key figures and body text. Screens are tested at an enlarged size.

Contrast

Theme colours are adjusted to keep enough contrast in light and dark mode.

Keyboard

Main actions have a shortcut (⌘N, ⌘K "Go to…", ⌘[ and ⌘], ⌥⌘Z) and the app can be driven from the keyboard.

VoiceOver and reduced motion

The app relies on standard macOS and iOS components, which support VoiceOver, and on the system's accessibility settings. Icon buttons carry a label.

On-screen privacy

Hiding amounts (⇧⌘H) also helps when using the app in public.

Status

The app's accessibility has not been audited externally: this is a self-assessment. I consider it partial and do not claim conformance with a specific standard.

Known limits

  • Some charts are visual first; their figures are also available as a table or an export.
  • Exported PDFs depend on what you enter.
  • I have not tested every VoiceOver combination on every screen.

This website: accessibility statement

The Pocket Ledger site is published by an individual on a non-professional basis: the French RGAA statement is not mandatory for them. I publish one anyway, for transparency.

Conformance status: not assessed against RGAA 4. This is a self-assessment with no external audit; I do not claim conformance.

What I checked: text contrast of at least 4.5:1 in light and dark mode; a skip link to the content; visible keyboard focus; the language of each page; a single main heading per page; alternative text on images; animations stopped when your system asks for it; a layout that reads well on a phone.

Content that is not accessible or not checked: the interactive simulation has not been tested with a screen reader; screenshots have a short description, not a full transcript; no complete automated check (such as axe) has been run.

Recourse: write to me first at atxgrp@gmail.com. If the answer is not satisfactory, you can contact the Défenseur des droits (defenseurdesdroits.fr).

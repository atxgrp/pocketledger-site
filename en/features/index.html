All features

Eight families, from the current account to the mortgage.

Your accounts

Everything you own, in one place.

  • Every kind of accountCurrent, card, cash, savings, investments, currencies with ECB rates.
  • A bank per accountPicked from a list of banks, with a badge, and asked at import.
  • Transfers and splitsBetween your accounts, or one expense shared across categories.
  • ClearingMark what is checked, or clear everything up to a date at once.
  • Tags and attachmentsA receipt or a contract kept with the transaction.
  • Receipt reading MacA scanned receipt is read on your Mac, nothing is sent.

Savings and investments

Savings books, life insurance, stocks: follow them like accounts.

  • Savings productsFrench savings books, PEA, life insurance, brokerage: ceiling, rate, interest.
  • Portfolio valueUpdate today's value: the difference becomes a gain or a loss.
  • Savings goalsAn amount, a date, the progress.
  • CryptoLots, cost basis, market prices and alerts.

Budget and forecast

Know what is left, and what is coming.

  • EnvelopesOne per category per month, with carry-over and pace alerts.
  • ForecastYour balance month by month: due items, detected subscriptions, loans, an estimate of everyday spending.
  • SubscriptionsDetected in your history, with price rises.
  • Recurring items and remindersAutomatic due items and notifications with buttons.

Housing and loans

What your home costs, and a loan that matches your bank's table.

  • HousingProperty type, rental or purchase with a loan, cost by item, share of income, net worth and tips.
  • Full scheduleInterest, capital, insurance, balance due at each date.
  • The banks' methodConstant total instalment, insurance as a % of the remaining capital.
  • Early repaymentShorten the term or lower the payment, compared side by side.
  • From a paymentThe app spots a regular debit and offers to create the loan.

Import

Your statements, no third-party service.

  • CSV assistantIt learns each bank's layout and lets you fix row by row.
  • Other formatsOFX, QFX, QIF, MT940, CAMT.053, XHB and Actual Budget.
  • PayPal and Trade RepublicPayPal purchases matched with no double counting; Trade Republic exports read in full.
  • Duplicates and undoDuplicates are flagged, an import is undone in one gesture.

Understand

What your data says.

  • Data analysisInternal transfers, duplicate categories, subscriptions, real balance of the last 12 months.
  • ReportsMonthly, trend, 50/30/20, financial health, PDF export.
  • Report builder MacCompose your own: period, categories, grouping.
  • PayeesNames cleaned, merged or kept apart, sorted as you like.

Protect and share

Your data, under your control.

  • LockTouch ID or Face ID, amounts hidden on screen.
  • Window hidden from captures MacThe app can hide itself from screenshots.
  • Verified backupsAutomatic copies on the device and iCloud; preview before restoring, integrity check, journal, undo.
  • Shared accountOne account, rights chosen per person, protected by signature.
  • SynciCloud, or an iCloud Drive, Dropbox or Syncthing folder.

Every day

Fewer taps for the same answers.

  • WidgetsNet worth, budgets, "Where the money goes" on the Home Screen or Desktop.
  • Siri and ShortcutsAdd an expense by voice.
  • Menu bar quick add MacAn expense in a few keystrokes, without opening the window.
  • Seven languagesFrench, English, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese.

Shared account

A joint account, precise rights.

Share a single account, such as a joint account, with rights chosen for each person.

What's inside

  • One account, not the whole ledger: only the accounts you choose are shared.
  • Three levels of rights: read only, enter (the person changes their own rows) or change everything.
  • Through iCloud: one profile per person (name, e-mail, rights), an "Invite by e-mail" button that prepares the message with the link; the person opens the link and the account appears on their device.
  • Or through a folder the other person can open (shared iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Syncthing). Either way, no publisher server.
  • Signatures: rights are protected by P-256 signatures; a file changed without the right is refused.
  • Activity: the Change journal shows what other people added or changed.
  • Keys: kept in the Keychain, restricted to your Mac.

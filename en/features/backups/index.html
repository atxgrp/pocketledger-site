Backups and integrity

Always able to go back.

Your data stays yours, and you can always go back.

What's inside

  • One automatic copy per day: 14 days then one per month, on this Mac.
  • Verified copies: each copy is read back after writing and carries a SHA-256 fingerprint; a damaged copy is flagged.
  • Preview before restoring: see what the backup contains; your current data is kept aside.
  • iCloud copy: your data can also be copied to iCloud and found on your other devices.
  • Integrity check: looks for damage and repairs it without losing anything.
  • Journal and undo: undo the last change or one specific change.
  • Erase: "Erase all my data" removes everything from this Mac.

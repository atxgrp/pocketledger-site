Reports and financial health

See where the money goes.

Understand where your money goes, over the period you choose.

What's inside

  • Ready-made reports: monthly, breakdown, 50/30/20 structure, trend, yearly, cash-flow projection, financial health and estimated carbon footprint.
  • Report builder: create your own report (grouping, measure, filters), save it and export it as CSV.
  • Free period: 12 months, 24 months or dates of your choice, filter by category.
  • PDF: print or export.
  • Subscriptions: recurring payments, their cost per month and per year.
  • Forecast: your balance month by month over 3 to 24 months, from your recurring items, regular payments, loans and an estimate of everyday spending.
  • Data analysis: transfers to link, categories to merge, price rises, the real 12-month balance, suggested budgets.
  • Widget: "Where the money goes" shows the month's breakdown on the Desktop.
  • Privacy: amounts and charts hide with one click.

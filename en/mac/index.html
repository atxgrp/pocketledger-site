What the Mac adds

  • Statement import: CSV assistant, OFX, QIF, MT940, CAMT.053, PayPal, Trade Republic.
  • Reports and builder: compose your own reports and export them as PDF.
  • Menu bar and keyboard shortcuts: add an expense without opening the window.
  • Privacy: Touch ID, hidden amounts, window hidden from screen captures.
  • Receipt reading on your Mac, nothing sent.

Touch-screen MacBook Pro

Touch, drag, type.

On a touch-screen MacBook Pro, Pocket Ledger can also be used with a finger, with nothing changed for the mouse or the keyboard.

  • Larger touch targets: icons at least 32 points, taller capsule buttons, taller rows in the account list.
  • Same app, same look: with a pointer, nothing changes.
  • Keyboard and shortcuts: the menu bar and shortcuts stay complete.
  • Still universal: Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Requirements

macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, Intel or Apple Silicon Mac.

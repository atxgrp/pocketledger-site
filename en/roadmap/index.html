Roadmap

What is delivered and what is under study.

Under study

  • iPhone and iPad version: under construction. Home, quick entry, transactions, budget, reports, import, shared accounts, crypto, widgets and Siri are written; they still need trying on real devices before a TestFlight beta.
  • More languages: further translations if people ask for them.
  • Suggestions: write to atxgrp@gmail.com to propose an idea; it will be discussed here.

Delivered in version 1.2.0

  • CSV import assistant, duplicates flagged, undoable batch.
  • Shared accounts with per-person rights.
  • Subscriptions detected in your history.
  • Attachments gathered, with search and sorting.
  • Fingerprinted backups, preview before restoring, verified copies.
  • Integrity check with repairs and a change journal.
  • Discovery mode with removable samples.
  • On-screen privacy: hidden amounts, window hidden from captures.
  • Adjustable text size and stronger contrast.
  • "To review" screen after an import and categories that follow your corrections.
  • Payee names cleaned up and a category suggested on import.
  • Notifications with buttons, loans compared, "Where the money goes" widget.
  • Automatic updates (off until you turn them on).

Security and privacy

Local first: your data stays with you.

Your financial data is personal. Pocket Ledger is built to keep it with you: nothing is sent to the publisher, and every protection below exists in the app.

Where your data lives

A readable file (ledger.json) and an attachments folder on your Mac. The file can only be read by your macOS user (permissions 600). A file newer than the app is never overwritten.

App lock

Touch ID or your Mac password, with a delay of your choice. The eye button (⇧⌘H) hides every amount, and you can hide them at launch or when the app goes to the background. One option hides the window from screen sharing and captures.

Keys and secrets

Bank access keys and your shared-account signing keys are kept in your Mac's Keychain, restricted to this device, never in the data file.

Verified backups

One automatic copy per day (14 days, then one per month), read back after writing and refused if it does not match. Each backup carries a SHA-256 fingerprint: a modified or damaged copy is refused on restore, and a preview shows before restoring. Backups can be password-encrypted (AES-256-GCM); without the password nobody, ATX Grp included, can read them.

Integrity check

Manage → Integrity check looks for damage (duplicates, missing categories, orphan transfers…) and repairs it without losing anything; a repair can be undone. A change journal, kept on this Mac, can undo one specific change.

Shared accounts

Each person's rights (read, enter, change everything) are protected by P-256 signatures: a file changed by someone without the right is refused.

Network

The app only connects to what you turn on: ECB exchange rates, crypto prices, bank connection with your own keys, update checks. Updates are signed (Sparkle, Ed25519 signature) and off until you turn them on. No tracking, no advertising SDK.

Signed and notarised app

The app is signed with a Developer ID, notarised by Apple and uses the Hardened Runtime. Test hooks are absent from the shipped version.

Erase your data

Settings → Your data → Erase all my data deletes the file, attachments, backups and journal from this Mac.

Report a vulnerability

Write to atxgrp@gmail.com describing the problem and how to reproduce it. Please allow a reasonable delay before any public disclosure. See also /.well-known/security.txt.

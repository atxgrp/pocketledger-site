A budget app made by one person, to keep your accounts with you.

Pocket Ledger is a personal project. I make it on a non-professional basis, with no investor and no ads.

I wrote it from scratch for Mac, iPhone and iPad. Data sources from other people (European Central Bank rates, cryptocurrency prices) are credited on the Credits page.

The app exists in seven languages: French, English, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch and Portuguese.

Every release is tested

More than 230 automated tests check the calculations (balances, loans, imports, sync). A check also runs on the build and on the texts before every release.

Free

Pocket Ledger has no paid tier, no ads and sells no data.

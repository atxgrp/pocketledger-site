About

Free apps, published by ATX Grp.

Pocket Ledger is a free, ad-free budget and accounts app for Mac, iPhone and iPad, published by ATX Grp. It is inspired by the simplicity of classic money managers but written from scratch: its code, texts and data are our own.

Our principles

Privacy first

Your data stays on your device. No account to create, no publisher server, no tracking.

Real Apple apps

Written in SwiftUI for Mac, iPhone and iPad, with widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, notifications and the Keychain.

Verified work

Over a hundred and forty automated tests of the calculation core, a quality check before every release (texts, identity, tests, build, extreme data on every screen, stability) and seven languages: French, English, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese (Brazil).

Free

Pocket Ledger has no paid tier, no ads and sells no data.

An independent publisher

ATX Grp is an independent publisher, with no investor and no monetisation of your data. Contact: atxgrp@gmail.com.

Legal

The legal notice, privacy policy, terms of use and credits are published on this site.

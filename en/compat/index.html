Which devices?

Pocket Ledger runs on recent Macs, iPhones and iPads, with the same iCloud sync.

Mac

macOS 14 Sonoma or later

  • Apple silicon (M1 and later) and Intel Macs: the app is universal
  • Touch-screen MacBook Pro: larger touch targets
  • Touch ID, menu bar, Desktop widgets, Siri and Shortcuts

iPhone

iOS 17 or later

  • Every iPhone that supports iOS 17, from iPhone XS
  • iPhone Duo: layout adapted to both displays
  • Face ID or Touch ID, widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, the camera for receipts

iPad

iPadOS 17 or later

  • Every iPad that supports iPadOS 17
  • A layout made for the big screen
  • Face ID or Touch ID, widgets, Siri and Shortcuts

On all your devices

  • iCloud: the ledger and its attachments follow your devices signed in with the same Apple ID.
  • Seven languages: French, English, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese.
  • Appearance and legibility: dark mode, system text size, VoiceOver (see Accessibility).
